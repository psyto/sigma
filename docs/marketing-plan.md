# Sigma Marketing & Promotion Plan

## Executive Summary

Sigma is entering a market where most DeFi users don't understand derivatives. Our marketing strategy must **educate first, sell second**. We're not competing on features alone—we're creating a new category in DeFi.

## Marketing Phases

### Phase 1: Pre-Launch (4-6 weeks before devnet)

**Goal:** Build awareness, grow community, establish thought leadership

#### Content Strategy

| Week | Content Type | Topics |
|------|-------------|--------|
| 1-2 | Educational threads | "What are variance swaps?", "Funding rates explained" |
| 3-4 | Problem-focused | "Why options are too expensive", "The funding rate trap" |
| 5-6 | Solution tease | "A better way to trade volatility", "Coming soon to Solana" |

#### Channels

- **Twitter/X**: 3-5 tweets/day, 2 threads/week
- **Discord**: Launch server, start community
- **Telegram**: Announcement channel
- **Medium**: 1 long-form article/week

#### Tactics

1. **Educational Thread Series**
   - "Derivatives 101" - Explain each instrument simply
   - "TradFi vs DeFi" - What Wall Street has that we don't
   - "Real trader stories" - Pain points with current tools

2. **Community Building**
   - Discord with roles: Trader, LP, Developer, OG
   - Early access list for devnet
   - AMAs with team

3. **Influencer Seeding**
   - Identify 20-30 Solana/DeFi influencers
   - Send pitch deck + early access
   - Offer exclusive content/interviews

### Phase 2: Devnet Launch (2-4 weeks)

**Goal:** Get users testing, collect feedback, build hype

#### Launch Tactics

1. **Devnet Campaign**
   - "Test & Earn" - Rewards for devnet users
   - Bug bounty program
   - Feedback rewards (quality feedback = whitelist)

2. **Content Blitz**
   - Launch video (use pitch deck as base)
   - Tutorial series for each protocol
   - "First trade" challenge on Twitter

3. **PR Push**
   - Press release to crypto media
   - Exclusive story to 1-2 top outlets
   - Podcast appearances

#### Metrics to Track

- Discord members
- Devnet users
- Transactions on devnet
- Social mentions
- Email signups

### Phase 3: Mainnet Beta (4-8 weeks)

**Goal:** Acquire users, bootstrap liquidity, prove product-market fit

#### Launch Tactics

1. **LP Incentive Program**
   - Early LP bonus (higher share of fees)
   - LP leaderboard
   - Referral rewards

2. **Trader Acquisition**
   - Trading competitions
   - "First profitable trade" stories
   - Partnerships with trading communities

3. **Integration Marketing**
   - Announce integrations (wallets, aggregators)
   - Co-marketing with partners
   - Developer documentation push

### Phase 4: Growth (Ongoing)

**Goal:** Sustain growth, expand to new audiences, increase TVL

#### Ongoing Tactics

1. **Content Engine**
   - Weekly market commentary using Sigma tools
   - Monthly "State of Derivatives" report
   - User success stories

2. **Community Programs**
   - Ambassador program
   - Content creator grants
   - Meme competitions

3. **Partnerships**
   - CEX listings (token, if applicable)
   - Integration with other DeFi protocols
   - Institutional outreach

## Campaign Ideas

### Campaign 1: "The Funding Rate Challenge"

**Concept:** Show traders how much they've lost to funding

**Execution:**
1. Build calculator: "How much did funding cost you?"
2. Users input their perp positions
3. Shows total funding paid over time
4. CTA: "Lock in your rate with FundingSwap"

**Distribution:**
- Twitter thread introducing problem
- Calculator tool (simple web page)
- User screenshots showing their losses
- Influencer participation

### Campaign 2: "Predict the Vol"

**Concept:** Gamified prediction market for variance

**Execution:**
1. Weekly prediction game
2. Users predict: "Will SOL variance be above or below X%?"
3. Winners get prizes/whitelist spots
4. Educates users about variance trading

### Campaign 3: "Crash Insurance Sale"

**Concept:** Time-limited promotion on barrier puts

**Execution:**
1. "Black Friday for Crash Protection"
2. Reduced premiums for knock-in puts
3. Limited time, creates urgency
4. Partner with whale wallets to show adoption

### Campaign 4: "LP Olympics"

**Concept:** Competition for liquidity providers

**Execution:**
1. 4-week competition
2. Categories: Highest APY, Most Consistent, Best Risk-Adjusted
3. Prizes for top LPs
4. Leaderboard creates social proof
